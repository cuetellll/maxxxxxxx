Mahyar Music — the shared music bot

Like a real Discord music bot, Mahyar Music joins your voice room as its own participant and plays one shared audio source to everyone. Every listener keeps an independent personal volume.

  • Shared music library — upload your own MP3/FLAC files and play them together
  • /mp play searches the library with an interactive picker
  • Shared queue with pause, resume, skip and stop for everyone
  • Personal 0–200% volume and personal mute — only for you
  • A real bot identity in the voice participant list

FAQ

Questions people usually ask

Who is Mahyar Gap for?
Private small groups — a circle of friends who want their own space: light, fast and ad-free.
How does the music bot work?
Mahyar Music joins the LiveKit voice room as a real participant and publishes one shared audio track. Each user controls their own local volume and can mute the music for themselves only.
Which music sources are supported?
Only sources that officially and explicitly permit shared playback. If a source (like Spotify) does not allow group retransmission, the app says so honestly instead of bypassing it.
How do I create an account?
Click "Get Started", pick a username and email, and you are in. To join your friends' server, open their invite link.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes — the public pages and the app itself are fully responsive for phones, tablets and desktops.
How do I contact the team?
Mahyar Gap is a private product; for anything administrative, reach out to your server's owner or admin.

Build your own space

It takes a couple of minutes: create an account, invite your friends and play your first shared song.
