Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Mahyar Gap is a private service for a small circle of friends. This page explains, in plain words, what data is stored and why.

Data we store

  • Account: username, display name, email, optional avatar, and a password hash (your password is never stored in plain text).
  • Content: channel and direct messages, uploaded attachments, and server/channel settings.
  • Technical state: online presence and active voice sessions so rooms can show who is connected.

Data we do not store

  • Voice and music are never recorded; media flows directly through LiveKit.
  • Music queue and playback state live in server memory only and are never written to the database.
  • There are no ads, analytics trackers or advertising cookies.

Third-party services

Media transport runs through LiveKit. If a music source is enabled (e.g. SoundCloud through its official API), metadata requests are made server-side and no personal data is shared with that service.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time, or ask your server admin to remove your content. For anything else, contact your server owner.
